    Collapsing
    When the mouse leaves, Floatie shrinks to just today's tasks. Rest the mouse on it (or click it) to open it again. Typing in it, or an open menu, keeps it open. Turn this off with the arrows button in the header.
    Tasks
    Right-click a task for every option. Double-click to rename. Drag to reorder. Unfinished tasks roll over to today. Everything you do is listed in History, where deleted tasks can be restored.
    Clipboard
    Everything you copy is kept in Recent (last 300). Click a clip to copy it again. Move a clip into a folder to keep it forever. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it.
    Keyboard
    Ctrl+Alt+Space show / hide from anywhere (Ctrl = Cmd on Mac)
    Ctrl+K or / new task · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+H history
    Alt+← Alt+→ previous / next day · T today · W calendar
    Ctrl+1 Ctrl+2 Ctrl+3 tabs · Ctrl+M collapse now · Esc minimize (click it in the taskbar or the Desktop icon to bring it back)
