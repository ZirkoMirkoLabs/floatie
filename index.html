      Collapsing
      When the mouse leaves, Floatie shrinks to just today's tasks. Rest the mouse on it (or click it) to open it again. Typing in it, or an open menu, keeps it open. Turn this off with the arrows button in the header.
      Tasks
      Right-click a task for every option. Double-click to rename. Drag to reorder. Unfinished tasks roll over to today. Everything you do is listed in History, where deleted tasks can be restored.
      Clipboard
      Everything you copy is kept in Recent, newest first by day (how many: Settings → Keep in Recent). Click a clip to copy it again. Move a clip into a folder to keep it forever. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it, or an image to save it as a file. The camera button takes a screenshot straight onto the clipboard; ⌘⇧⌃4Win+Shift+S works too.
      Snippets
      Save text you type again and again (your address, email, a sign-off) under a short keyword. Press Alt+Shift+V in any app, type the keyword (Tab completes it) and press Enter: it's copied and you're back where you were, ready to paste.
